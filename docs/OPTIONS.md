# Level 2 options, telemetry and lifecycle

Only single-leg LONG_CALL, LONG_PUT, COVERED_CALL and CASH_SECURED_PUT are modeled. Integral contracts use verified plain multiplier 100. Covered calls require 100 owned same-sleeve shares per contract; CSPs reserve full strike ×100 collateral. Shorts without coverage, Level 3/multileg/adjusted-deliverable inference and borrowing fail closed. Broker options approval/access must be verified independently on the real Agentic account.

Risk settings include strategy allowlist, long-call/put flags, DTE, contracts/concurrency/premium/sleeve risk, OI/volume/spread, underlying liquidity, optional delta/IV/theta/vega exposure, total premium and collateral. Trusted facts determine these values; proposal estimates cannot establish coverage or maximum loss. Required unavailable facts reject risk, rather than being manufactured by the model.

`option_telemetry` records observed trusted quote/instrument facts on risk calls, with provenance and timestamps. Missing Greeks/IV/IV rank/underlying observations remain null. UI offers open and closed ownership history, contract and underlying charts, confirmed-fill entry/add/partial/final-close plus explicit economic-lifecycle timeline, duration and recorded per-contract mark P&L versus paired underlying observations. The professional summary promotes contracts, entry/current mark, bid/ask/spread, volume/open interest, maximum loss when recorded on the opening proposal, premium paid/received, realized/economic and unrealized P&L, collateral and break-even; expanded detail retains actual proposal thesis/context/exit plan, risk decisions, lifecycle, fills and execution/broker IDs. Synthetic fixture Greeks are explicitly marked synthetic, not estimates for live contracts. Price history combines recorded quote marks and confirmed execution prices, never fabricates a price for an expiration/assignment/exercise event, and never fabricates historical ticks.

Plain expiration/assignment/exercise are explicit reviewed broker economic events; zero-value expired contracts use economic_event_pnl, never an invalid zero-price fill. Physical share/cash movement and premium resolution are atomic, idempotent and source-reviewed. This is **not complete broker tax accounting**: adjusted contracts, external option trade imports, dividend-related corporate events, exercise tax-basis roll-in and reliable live IV rank remain uncommissioned. Reconciliation stays paused on unexplained events. Automatic thesis/ATR/percent stops and trade management are not implemented; use separately researched/risk-reviewed/previewed/manual-approved exits.
